# MediMart vs Tata 1mg: Product Gap Analysis

Audit date: 12 October 2026

This is a functional comparison, not a recommendation to copy Tata 1mg's branding,
content, data, or visual trade dress. MediMart should keep its own identity and obtain
the necessary licences, contracts, medical review, and legal approvals before launch.

## Deep Tata 1mg Surface Inventory

Review date: 12 October 2026

The following is an inventory of public Tata 1mg surfaces and observable workflows. It is
intended as a coverage checklist for MediMart, not a request to copy Tata 1mg's branding,
copy, imagery, layout, or proprietary implementation. The detailed P0/P1/P2 sections below
track the engineering and compliance work needed to provide an equivalent capability safely.

### Entry, Navigation, and Discovery

- Delivery city or pincode selection, location persistence, and serviceability-aware catalog.
- Separate entry points for medicines, health products, lab tests, doctor consultations, and
  the Care Plan membership.
- Search across medicines, health products, brands, conditions, symptoms, tests, packages,
  and health information, with autocomplete and result refinement.
- Home-page merchandising for health concerns, popular categories, brands, offers, products,
  lab packages, vaccinations, and seasonal or lifestyle needs.
- Catalog filters for relevance, rating, price, discount, brand, use/concern, product form,
  age, gender, country of origin, availability, and delivery promise.
- Product cards showing images, pack/form, ratings and review counts, MRP and selling price,
  discount, offer eligibility, delivery estimate, bestseller/ad labels, and add-to-cart.
- Location-specific price, inventory, delivery speed, and minimum-order or coupon eligibility.
- First-order/app promotion surfaces, sign-in/sign-up, account menu, cart, orders, saved
  items, support, and policy/footer links.

### E-Pharmacy and Health Products

- Allopathic medicines, generic medicines, prescription medicines, OTC products, vitamins,
  supplements, nutrition drinks, personal care, skin and hair care, baby care, mother care,
  women's care, men's care, sexual wellness, dental care, first aid, and daily-living aids.
- Ayurveda, homeopathy, herbal products, immunity products, condition-oriented wellness,
  and ingredient/herb discovery with separate safety and legal review requirements.
- Healthcare devices and diagnostic aids such as glucometers, strips, blood-pressure devices,
  thermometers, respiratory devices, mobility aids, surgical aids, masks, bandages, and
  assisted-living products.
- Category pages with top picks, featured products, bestsellers, brands, uses, forms, and
  rich facet counts; sort options include relevance, rating, low/high price, and discount.
- Medicine/product detail pages with composition or ingredients, strength, form, uses,
  benefits, directions, dosage, side effects, warnings, interactions, storage, substitutes,
  manufacturer/marketer, pack sizes, MRP, selling price, offers, ratings, reviews, FAQs,
  prescription requirement, delivery promise, seller/pharmacy information, and related items.
- Prescription upload, prescription-only product controls, licensed pharmacy fulfillment,
  pharmacist review, customer clarification, and delivery-time prescription safeguards.
- Product variants and pack sizes, quantity limits, stock visibility, add-to-cart, saved
  items, recently viewed products, alternatives, price comparison, and buy-again flows.
- Cart and checkout with address selection, contact details, delivery charges, handling or
  platform charges, coupon application, payment options, COD rules, order review, and
  customer-visible reconciliation or payment-failure handling.
- Order confirmation, email/app notifications, invoice, shipment tracking, delivery proof,
  cancellation rules, return/refund policy, support escalation, and reorder experience.
- Delivery promises ranging from standard delivery to rapid or same-day delivery in selected
  cities, subject to pincode, inventory, cutoff, warehouse, seller, and courier capacity;
  the public platform describes broad multi-city coverage and faster delivery in selected cities.
- Care Plan benefits presented alongside commerce, including a free consultation, savings,
  free shipping, rapid delivery, and premium support.
- Retail pharmacy and hospital-pharmacy relationships in addition to online pharmacy
  fulfillment, plus corporate-health or employee wellness offerings.

### Account, Commerce, and Support Operations

- Account registration/login, profile, verified contact details, saved addresses, family or
  dependent care, order history, invoices, prescriptions, reports, consultations, saved
  products, notifications, and privacy/communication preferences.
- Account recovery, optional MFA, session/device controls, suspicious-login handling, and
  protected access to medical documents and consultation data.
- Coupons, first-order offers, category promotions, minimum-order offers, bank/payment offers,

  cancellation, return request, damage/wrong/near-expiry handling, reverse pickup, inspection,
  replacement or refund, refund timing, and dispute escalation. The reviewed public return
  policy describes a seven-day return request window, wrong/damaged/near-expiry handling, and

  internal ownership, SLA/escalation, complaint history, and grievance handling.
- Service notifications for order, payment, refund, consultation, lab booking, collection,
  report, delivery, prescription review, stock, and policy events through enabled channels.

### Trust, Compliance, and Platform Signals

- Verified/licensed pharmacy and partner claims, product authenticity messaging, prescription
  safeguards, controlled-product restrictions, and privacy/security messaging.
- Public claims around delivery reach, orders, cities, accredited labs, qualified doctors,
  LegitScript or ISO certification, and compliance with Indian medicines law; each MediMart
  claim must be independently substantiated before publication.
- Explicit exclusion of Schedule X and habit-forming drug requests on the public pharmacy
  surface, plus rules for prescription medicines and prohibited online sales.
- Responsive web and app-oriented journeys, image-rich merchandising, accessible content,
  location-aware pricing/availability, app acquisition offers, push or account notifications,
  performance monitoring, analytics, and controlled personalization.
